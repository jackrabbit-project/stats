2009 - 2016 Records

The 2026 ACoD delegates recognized Lori Zorbaugh for 25 years of service as the Records Coordinator for ASFA.
     Lori took over the ASFA Records Coordinator position in 2000 from Camille Mendicino. Camille had taken the ASFA into the computerized age, but Lori saw us through several iterations of Records programs. She is still seeing us through an updated records program. She has spent myriad hours validating records from field trials, answering our questions, sending reports to the website, and sending out countless hound records and certificates.
There are not enough words to say THANK YOU to Lori.

Trial Result Codes
Stake (#, 2NQ, Dis/DQ) - the first number indicates the number of hounds competing for points and determines the number of points awarded in that stake
The numbers following indicate hounds which are non-qualifying, dismissed/disqualified and "NC" denotes those hounds that were absent, pulled before running the preliminary run, were excused or received an -0- during the prelininary run and do not count when calculating points.
B = Best of Breed
R = Runoff
T = Tie
E = Excused
F = Runoff Forfeited
(hound name) = hound is ineligible to be entered in that stake. Points/ placements remain the same for all other hounds in that stake.
