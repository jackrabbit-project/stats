How this is built

What this is

ASFA publishes its Top 20 lure coursing standings as one long page: two dozen breed tables stacked end to end, refreshed every few weeks during the season. Everything you need is in there, but you cannot search it, you cannot look up a single hound, and you cannot see how a hound stacks up against the rest of its breed without counting by hand.

This site reads that page and rearranges it — nothing more. No figure here is calculated from anything ASFA has not already published.

Where the numbers come from

A single source: the ASFA Top 20 standings page. Each time ASFA publishes an update, the page is archived verbatim in this project's repository, so every number on this site can be traced back to the bytes ASFA served on a given date.

The parser refuses to publish if the page shape changes: it checks that every row has eight columns, that ranks form a valid tie-aware sequence within each breed, and that no breed lists more hounds than ASFA reports competing. A silent parsing failure would quietly corrupt every leaderboard here, so it fails loudly instead.

What each column means

Top 20 points
Points earned under the Bowen system, which scores a hound on the competition it beats rather than on wins alone. ASFA's own explainer is published here.
BOB — Best of Breed
Wins over the rest of the breed at a trial.
BIF — Best in Field
Wins over every hound at the trial, across all breeds. Rare — most hounds never earn one, and most breeds record none in a season.
Region
The ASFA region on the hound's row.
Breed standing
This site's only derived figure: the hound's rank divided by the number of hounds ASFA reports competing in that breed. Ranked 4th of 25 is top 16%.

What these numbers cannot tell you

Worth reading before you draw a conclusion from anything here.

  1. A Top 20 list holds about twenty hounds per breed. A hound placing 22nd is absent entirely. Every total on this site covers placings on that list — it is not a complete competition record.
  2. Points do not compare across breeds. A breed with 200 hounds competing offers far more points than one with five. Comparing a Whippet's point total to an Azawakh's says more about breed size than about either hound. Breed standing and Best in Field are the figures that compare fairly.
  3. The Singles stake overlaps the breed lists. Hounds that run Singles are also listed under their breed, and both rows can carry the same BIF and BOB. Where this site combines sections it takes the maximum of each figure rather than the sum, so a record can never be inflated by being counted twice. Headline population counts leave the Singles stake out for the same reason.
  4. Standings are provisional. ASFA marks entries with pending information that may change a rank; those are flagged here as they are published.
  5. Matching a hound across updates is approximate. There is no registration number in the published data, and a registered name changes during the season as titles are earned. This site strips titles to match a hound to itself between updates. It works almost always; it is not infallible.
  6. Owner grouping is approximate. Owners are free text, and the same household is spelled several ways. Spellings are merged where the match is mechanical. A co-owned hound counts for every owner named on the row, so owner totals sum to more than the season total.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, please check it against ASFA's published standings first. If the two disagree, ASFA is right and this site needs fixing — that is a bug worth reporting. If ASFA's own listing looks wrong, that goes to the ASFA Records Secretary, not here.
