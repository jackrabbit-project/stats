How this is built

What this is

ASFA publishes its Top 20 standings as one massive page of breed tables stacked end to end. The data is all there, but it's hard to use: you can't search for a specific hound or easily see how they rank against their breed without counting rows by hand.

This site fixes that by taking ASFA's published page and making it searchable and sortable. That's it. Every number here comes directly from ASFA's official publications. The one figure we work out ourselves is standing: a hound's rank against the number ASFA reports competing in its breed.

Where the numbers come from

Five official ASFA publications, and nothing else:

  • The Top 20 standings page: Every hound, rank, and figure on this site.
  • The monthly trial results: Entry counts per trial, used on the Regions page.
  • The trial schedule: Dates, clubs, venues and premium lists, shown on the Events page.
  • The LCI Rules (rev. 08/01/2024): Division definitions, scoring, and titles for the Lure Chasing Instinct program, used on the LCI page. (For reference only; no standings are derived from it).
  • The club listing: Identifies which region each club belongs to.

Understanding the columns

Top 20 points
Calculated using the Bowen system, which awards a hound one point for placing plus one for every hound it defeated, rather than scoring placements alone. How it works, with a calculator, or read ASFA's official explainer here.
BOB — Best of Breed
The number of times the hound won over the rest of its breed at a trial. Worth knowing that the count says nothing about how many it beat: where a breed has only one entry that day, that hound takes Best of Breed on its own qualifying score, having defeated nobody (Running Rules Ch. V §7).
BIF — Best in Field
The top award at a trial, won in a single run among that day's Best of Breed winners. This is incredibly rare; most hounds never earn one, and many breeds will see none in a given season.
Region
The specific ASFA region assigned to the hound.
Standing
The only metric calculated by this site. It shows the hound's percentile by dividing its rank by the total number of hounds competing in that breed (4th out of 25 means the top 16%).

What these numbers cannot tell you

  • It is a Top 20, not a full record. A hound placing 22nd in its breed is absent entirely.
  • Points do not compare across breeds. A breed with 200 hounds competing offers far more points than one with five. Standing and Best in Field are the figures that compare fairly.
  • Best of Breed and Best in Field are breed-stake awards. ASFA prints them on Singles rows too — that is the hound's breed record carried across, so they are never counted toward Singles or LCI here.
  • Region means the owner's in the standings, the club's on trial entries. The two are never added together.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, please check it against ASFA's published standings first. If the two disagree, ASFA is right and this site needs fixing — that is a bug worth reporting. If ASFA's own listing looks wrong, that goes to the ASFA Records Secretary, not here.
