How this is built

What this is

ASFA publishes its Top 20 standings as one massive page of breed tables stacked end to end. The data is all there, but it's hard to use: you can't search for a specific hound or easily see how they rank against their breed without counting rows by hand.

This site fixes that by taking ASFA's published page and making it searchable and sortable. That's it. Every number here comes directly from ASFA's official publications. The one figure we work out ourselves is standing: a hound's rank against the number ASFA reports competing in its breed.

Where the numbers come from

Four official ASFA publications, and nothing else:

  • The Top 20 standings page: Every hound, rank, and figure on this site.
  • The monthly trial results: Entry counts per trial, used on the Regions page.
  • The LCI Rules (rev. 08/01/2024): Division definitions, scoring, and titles for the Lure Chasing Instinct program, used on the LCI page. (For reference only; no standings are derived from it).
  • The club listing: Identifies which region each club belongs to.

Understanding the columns

Top 20 points
Calculated using the Bowen system, which awards a hound one point for placing plus one for every hound it defeated, rather than scoring placements alone. How it works, with a calculator, or read ASFA's official explainer here.
BOB — Best of Breed
The number of times the hound won over the rest of its breed at a trial. Worth knowing that the count says nothing about how many it beat: where a breed has only one entry that day, that hound takes Best of Breed on its own qualifying score, having defeated nobody (Running Rules Ch. V §7).
BIF — Best in Field
The top award at a trial, won in a single run among that day's Best of Breed winners. This is incredibly rare; most hounds never earn one, and many breeds will see none in a given season.
Region
The specific ASFA region assigned to the hound.
Standing
The only metric calculated by this site. It shows the hound's percentile by dividing its rank by the total number of hounds competing in that breed (4th out of 25 means the top 16%).

What these numbers cannot tell you

Worth reading before you draw a conclusion from anything here.

  1. A Top 20 list holds about twenty hounds per breed. A hound placing 22nd is absent entirely. Every total on this site covers placings on that list — it is not a complete competition record.
  2. Points do not compare across breeds. A breed with 200 hounds competing offers far more points than one with five. Comparing a Whippet's point total to an Azawakh's says more about breed size than about either hound. Standing and Best in Field are the figures that compare fairly.
  3. The Singles stake overlaps the breed lists. Hounds that run Singles are also listed under their breed, and both rows can carry the same BIF and BOB. Where this site combines sections it takes the maximum of each figure rather than the sum, so a record can never be inflated by being counted twice. Headline population counts leave the Singles stake out for the same reason.
  4. Standings are provisional. ASFA marks entries with pending information that may change a rank; those are flagged here as they are published.
  5. Matching a hound across updates is approximate. There is no registration number in the published data, and a registered name changes during the season as titles are earned. This site strips titles to match a hound to itself between updates. It works almost always; it is not infallible.
  6. Owner grouping is approximate. Owners are free text, and the same household is spelled several ways. Spellings are merged where the match is mechanical. A co-owned hound counts for every owner named on the row, so owner totals sum to more than the season total.
  7. Two people can share a name. ASFA publishes no owner identifier, so this site works out where an owner is based from the rows where they are listed first — that is whose region the row carries. A name that leads in two regions is treated as two people: K.Sanders has six Basenjis in Region 8 and a Silken Windhound in Region 1. A name that leads in two regions but shares a co-owner across them is treated as one person whose hounds live in different places. Where the evidence is thin the split is kept, because inventing a combined record for two real people is the worse mistake.
  8. Best of Breed and Best in Field are breed-stake awards only. Hounds run alone in the Singles stake, whose winner "shall not be eligible to compete in Best of Breed nor in Best in Field" (Running Rules Ch. V §5(d)); Ch. V §11 excludes Singles hounds from Best in Field outright, and Best of Breed is contested between the breed stakes, which the LCI divisions are not. ASFA nonetheless prints BOB and BIF figures on Singles rows — they are that hound's breed-stake record carried across, which is why the Saluki "Onyx" and the Singles entry "SA Onyx" show the same six of each. Those figures are shown here as published, but never counted toward Singles or LCI on any leaderboard or total.
  9. Regions mean two different things. In the standings, a region is the owner's. On trial entries, it is the club's, taken from ASFA's club listing — clubs travel, and Borzoi Club of America is a Region 4 club that ran in Nebraska this year. The two are never added together.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, please check it against ASFA's published standings first. If the two disagree, ASFA is right and this site needs fixing — that is a bug worth reporting. If ASFA's own listing looks wrong, that goes to the ASFA Records Secretary, not here.
