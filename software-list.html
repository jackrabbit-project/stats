Dog Sport Events and Trial Software

Who actually runs the events. Entry platforms, trial secretary software, and owner-facing record keeping across North American dog sports.

Headline finding

No national dog sport organization appears to certify third-party trial software.

AKC, UKC, and CKC operate no software approval or certification program and publish no list of approved or certified vendors, and nothing of the kind turned up at the sanctioning organizations either. With one narrow exception — ASFA's Board has approved LCSoft's electronic draw, a single function — the commercial products below claim no approval from any of them, and several disclaim affiliation outright.

What exists instead:

  • AKC licenses superintendents — companies, not software — and only for conformation shows. Eight hold the license.
  • AKC publishes functional requirements for agility online entry systems. Requirements, not approvals: no vendor list, no audit, no seal. The club self-attests and names its provider in the premium list.
  • UKC and CKC went in-house — UKC with its own online entry service, CKC with its own event platform, Canuck Dogs — rather than approving anyone else's.
01

Organization-run systems

7

The only genuinely official software. Each organization built its own rather than certifying someone else's.

  • AKC Online Entry ServiceRegistry-run

    Official AKC online entry — real-time acceptance and wait-list status, pre-filled dog and owner data — with secretary-side entry downloads in trial-secretary database formats and a closing report.

    AKC agility · $2.00 first entry, $1.00 each additional · free to clubs

  • AKC Trial Secretary ToolsRegistry-run

    The secretary side of the entry service, inside My AKC — financial and entry reports exportable in five formats, and the random draw that decides which online entries are accepted when a trial oversubscribes, with an optional online waitlist. An admission draw, not a running-order draw.

    AKC agility · free to clubs with the Online Entry Service

  • UKC Online Event EntryRegistry-run

    UKC builds a custom entry page for each licensed club and handles the money and the entries.

    UKC all-breed sports, hunting programs following · free to clubs

  • Canuck DogsRegistry-run

    Canadian event listings, results, standings, and judge records. CKC results went live here in December 2025.

    CKC conformation, agility, obedience, rally, field trials, herding, lure coursing · free

  • Barn Hunt RegisterSanctioning body

    Dog registration, leg and title tracking, printable certificates, auto-filled entry forms, event search.

    Barn Hunt Association · US and Canada

  • NADAC Club PortalSanctioning body

    Trial applications and results submission through NADAC's own online forms. No third-party software named or required.

    NADAC agility

  • NAFA Flyball DatabaseSanctioning body

    Central NAFA records: tournament calendar, club and dog data, points, titles, height cards. The legacy FBScore scoring program is explicitly no longer supported — NAFA currently endorses no scoring software.

    NAFA flyball · free

Multi-sport entry and secretary platforms

8

Commercial. All uncertified by any registry — several say so on their own sites.

  • AKC.softwareCommercial

    Cloud event management — registrations, run orders, scoring, payments, reporting. Carries the clearest disclaimer of the group: "not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the American Kennel Club."

    AKC · USDAA · NADAC · CPE · TDAA · UKC · BHA · NACSW · NADD · NAFA — pay per entry, no contract

  • Eager DogCommercial

    Event, class, and seminar management with online entry and queue management. Claims 250+ clubs and 6,000+ owners.

    AKC · UKC · BHA · ABI · C-WAGS · CPE — conformation, obedience, rally, scent work, Fast CAT, barn hunt, herding, agility, dock, coursing

  • DogShow.comCommercial

    Continent-wide entry portal and secretary platform. Also the platform behind licensed superintendent Emerald Coast.

    AKC · UKC · BHA · International Silken Windhound Society · NASDA — includes UKC lure coursing · ~7% of entry fees

  • DogShow.caCommercial

    Canadian sibling of the above — entry, payments, waiting lists, catalogue generation, ringside app. Free to clubs; funded by a 10% exhibitor service charge.

    CKC · Agility Association of Canada · UKC · BHA · CARO and others — conformation, agility, obedience, rally, lure coursing, retriever trials, barn hunt

  • MyDogEntryCommercial

    Secretary dashboard covering online, paper, and waitlist entries, with run-order generation that separates same-handler dogs. Strongest agility-venue coverage here.

    AKC · USDAA · UKI · CPE · ASCA · NADAC · TDAA — $1 per online entry plus processing

  • PawPrint TrialsCommercial

    Entry, entry management, and real-time running orders on trial day. Roughly 35 AKC clubs and 150 trials a year; no added exhibitor fee.

    AKC agility, obedience, rally

  • ShowEntries.infoCommercial

    Online entry, premium lists, paper entry handling, judging programs, plus a full-service "Trial in a Box." Operating since 2007.

    AKC companion and performance — obedience, rally, scent work, herding, field trials · ~$175 setup, $100/yr

  • SecreterrierCommercial

    Online entry, trial management, queue management. Newest of the established set — founded November 2023.

    Scent work, barn hunt · $1.00 per class capped at $10 per checkout

  • Dog Title TrackerFreemium

    Tracks Qs, points, legs, placements, and title progress across several venues at once. States plainly that it is not affiliated with any dog sport organization.

    AKC · ASCA · CPE · UKI · USDAA · NADAC · AKC Fast CAT

  • Ruff TitleFreemium

    Mobile app for logging runs, tracking title progress, and storing dog profiles and registration numbers. Sport-agnostic.

    Any sport · free for 1 dog, $19.99 one-time for unlimited

  • Dash-HoundFree

    Handler-side Fast CAT tracker — runs logged by hand or imported from a CSV or an AKC-profile screenshot, with speed, points, and title progress computed to the AKC formula and a Best 3 Average projected against AKC’s own rankings. Includes a daily-updated finder for 1,000+ AKC Fast CAT events and breed leaderboards back to 2016.

    AKC Fast CAT only · free · web, iOS, Android · independent (Dash-Hound LLC)

  • UKC Points CheckRegistry-run

    Emailed report of a dog's accumulated points and title progress, back within about fifteen minutes — UKC’s automated per-dog lookup, covering its coursing programs.

    UKC all-breed sports including lure coursing and precision coursing, plus hunting programs · free · results lag events by ~3 weeks

  • AKC Points ProgressionRegistry-run

    Free points progression under your dog's details in the AKC account, with a fuller paid Points & Awards Report available by email.

    AKC · free tier plus paid report

  • AKC Event SearchRegistry-run

    Event calendar filtered by breed, judge, location, radius, and date, with club-uploaded premiums, running orders, and entry forms attached. AKC's de facto premium-list aggregator.

    All AKC sports · free

  • AgilityNerd Title & Event Goal TrackerFree

    Imports results and shows live progress toward AKC titles and toward event qualification.

    AKC agility · donation-ware

  • Bad Dog Agility rankingsFree

    Quarterly and annual national rankings — Power 60, Preferred Power 60, Breed Power 10 — computed from published trial results.

    Primarily AKC agility, some UKI and FCI · free to read

06

Lure coursing and racing

9
  • LCSoftCommercial

    The dedicated lure coursing secretary package — "a complete database solution for all Lure Coursing secretarial work." Stores trial data, performs true random draws, and prints judging, draw, score, and entry forms. Desktop Access application, no internet needed.

    AKC · AKC CAT · AKC Fast CAT · ASFA · CKC · ANKC — paid via trial credits · ASFA's Board has approved its electronic draw — an approval of that one function, and the closest thing to a software credential anywhere on this page

  • AOK9 Race SecretaryWeb appFree

    Browser-based race secretary application for the AOK9 program — R.A.C.E.'s all-breed sprint racing, open to sighthounds and non-sighthounds alike. Among the secretary packages built specifically for coursing and racing, the only web-native one — the rest are desktop software or paper.

    AOK9 / R.A.C.E. — all-breed sprint racing · free, no login · independent

  • Lure Coursing StatsVolunteer-run

    Per-dog search across ASFA competition — the per-dog query ASFA’s own pages don’t offer. BIF and BOB leaderboards, new titles, breed percentile rankings measured against every hound ASFA reports in that breed, kennel leaderboards, regional standings and entries, LCI standings by division. All statistics derive from ASFA’s published pages, checked weekly and re-verified on every refresh.

    ASFA lure coursing and LCI · free, no login · unofficial

  • Coursing ClubFree

    Fast CAT statistics — search by name rather than breed dropdowns, year-by-year speed history with lifetime averages, breed percentile rankings, head-to-head comparison, shareable stat cards. 80,721 dogs and 136,467 races indexed, sourced from AKC and updated weekly.

    AKC Fast CAT only · free · independent, states it is "not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the American Kennel Club"

  • ASFA records suiteSanctioning body

    Hound records, monthly trial results back to 2009, Top 20 standings by breed, and lifetime rankings — all static HTML updated monthly. Entries are PDF forms handled club by club; there is no national online entry portal.

    ASFA lure coursing · free, no login · records · results · Top 20

  • NOTRASanctioning body

    Race dates, points and grading guides, rulebook, and published standings — Whippet Top 10, Oval Racing Championship, breed SORCs. No per-dog lookup.

    Oval racing, US and Canada · free

  • LGRASanctioning body

    Race schedule, rule book, grading guide, and year-end standings — all-breed and by-breed top dogs, posted as spreadsheets. The same published-pages-and-forms pattern as NOTRA. No per-dog lookup.

    Straight racing, 200 yards · free

  • AOK9 RacingSanctioning body

    The sanctioning program's own site — calendar, rule book, entry forms, affiliated clubs, and a Hall of Fame of titled dogs. Sprint racing and lure coursing open beyond the sighthound breeds, on the same pattern as LGRA and NOTRA: published pages and forms, no online entry or per-dog lookup.

    AOK9 / R.A.C.E. — all-breed sprint racing and lure coursing · free

  • ASFA Region 1Volunteer-run

    Regional premium and flyer aggregator with an event calendar — the closest thing to a lure coursing premium list hub, but Pacific Northwest only.

    ASFA coursing and racing, AKC CAT/Fast CAT, AOK9 · WA, OR, ID, MT, AK, BC
