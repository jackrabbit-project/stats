The Bowen point system

Adopted in 1987, the Bowen system is how ASFA scores a hound for the Top 20 rankings — annually by breed, and for the lifetime standings. It is the source of every points figure on this site.

The basic rule

A hound earns one point for placing, plus one point for every hound it defeated. Which comes to:

points = (hounds in the stake − placement) + 1

Fourth place in a ten-hound stake is (10 − 4) + 1 = 7 points. The winner of a stake takes one point for every hound in it.

Only placing hounds earn Bowen points, and placements stop at NBQ — first through fourth, then Next Best Qualified. A hound finishing behind NBQ receives nothing, however many ran.

The rest of the rules

Stakes split into flights
The overall stake winner takes one point for every hound in the whole stake, not just their flight. A stake of three flights with ten hounds each pays the overall winner 30. The other flight winners receive nothing additional.
Best of Breed
The winner takes one point for every hound entered in that breed across all classes — whether the run happens or is forfeited. A breed entering a single hound therefore pays its Best of Breed winner one point.
Best in Field
Earns no additional Bowen points. The most a hound can take from a trial is the number of hounds entered in its breed.
Ties
Points are split evenly between the tied hounds. Only whole numbers are awarded; fractions are dropped.
Invalid entries
A hound entered invalidly scores nothing itself, but still counts as a hound defeated by everyone above it.

Why points don't compare between breeds

The whole scale is set by how many hounds turn up. A Whippet winning a stake of forty takes forty points; an Azawakh winning a stake of four takes four. Neither hound ran a better course than the other — one simply had more to beat.

That is why a point total means something within a breed and very little across breeds, and why this site leads with standing — a hound's rank against the number competing in its own breed — wherever the comparison crosses breeds.
